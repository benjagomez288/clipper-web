Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Clipper ("the app"), operated by Clipperproomax, is a personal desktop tool used only by its owner.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you also agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service, and Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

What information the app accesses

With the owner's authorization (Google OAuth), the app accesses the owner's own YouTube channel in order to upload videos (videos.insert), change the privacy status of the owner's own videos (videos.update) and read basic channel information (channels.list). On TikTok it uses the permissions user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish; on Instagram it uses the Instagram Graph API to publish Reels to the owner's own account.

How the information is used, stored and shared

This information is used only to publish the owner's own videos. Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer and are never shared with, sold or transferred to third parties. The app does not collect, store or process any data about other YouTube, TikTok or Instagram users, and it does not use cookies or tracking.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time on the Google security settings page (and in TikTok and Instagram settings). Deleting the local token files removes all stored data. To request deletion or ask questions, contact clipperchl123@gmail.com.
