Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 28, 2026

Clipperproomax ("the app") is a personal tool used only by its owner.

What the app accesses

The app connects only to the owner's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts, solely to upload videos as drafts or posts. On TikTok it uses the permissions user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish.

Storage and sharing

Access tokens are stored only on the owner's computer. They are never shared with or sold to third parties. The app does not collect, store or process data from any other TikTok, YouTube or Instagram users.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in each platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings → Security → Manage app permissions).
